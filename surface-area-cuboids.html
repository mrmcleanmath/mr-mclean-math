About this activity

Find the surface area of a cuboid, also called a rectangular prism, by adding the areas of all six faces. Use 2(lw + lh + wh), then check the square units.

Example and related practice

For l = 4 cm, w = 3 cm and h = 2 cm: surface area = 2(12 + 8 + 6) = 52 cm².
