AREA OF CIRCLES

Instructions: Calculate circle areas. Foundation uses small whole-number radii and nearest-whole rounding. Core adds diameters and rounds to the nearest whole number or tenth. Challenge adds decimal measurements, odd diameters, occasional reverse area questions, and rounding to the nearest whole number, tenth, or hundredth. Use π = 3.14 and enter the number only. Get 3 correct answers to unlock the next level.
