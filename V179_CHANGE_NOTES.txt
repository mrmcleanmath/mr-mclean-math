V179 — Embedded mobile answer pads across practice apps

- Replaced the floating/replacement-style mobile keypad with a persistent answer pad embedded directly inside the active practice card on mobile phones.
- The phone's native keyboard remains suppressed for configured practice answers, preventing it from consuming most of the screen.
- Each practice app receives only the keys its mathematics needs:
  - whole-number apps: digits 0–9 plus edit/check controls;
  - decimal apps: digits plus decimal point;
  - probability: digits plus fraction slash, decimal point, and percent;
  - algebra: digits plus signs and the variables a, c, x, y;
  - fraction-entry apps: digit pad targets the active whole/numerator/denominator field;
  - prime factorization keeps its existing prime-factor buttons and receives only Backspace, Clear, and Enter editing controls.
- Embedded Enter preserves the normal Check -> Enter -> Next workflow after both correct and incorrect answers.
- The shared system applies to all 13 normal practice apps currently listed on the Practice page, including Division with Decimals and Convert a Fraction.
- Pi Memory Challenge retains its own purpose-built embedded numeric keypad and does not use the shared practice pad.
- The embedded answer pads are phone-only; desktop/laptop inputs and hardware keyboard behavior are unchanged.
