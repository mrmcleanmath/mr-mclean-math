Prices are examples for Sweden. Housing already includes home insurance. Car costs already include fuel. The used-car price includes buying the car in the first year. Savings are money you keep, not spend.

For teachers: About the prices

Useful price sources: SCB: Swedish rent averages (2026) · Konsumentverket: Food (2026) · Länstrafiken Jämtland: tickets · Bredbandsval: internet · Spotify prices · SCB: Mortgage rates.

Important: These prices help students practise budgeting. They are not real offers. Bedroom counts mean actual bedrooms. Some homes include electricity. For home loans, students are using simple first-year examples, without a down payment. The app works in the browser and does not send the budget to a server.
