About this activity

Use speed = distance ÷ time, distance = speed × time, or time = distance ÷ speed. Choose a focus and practise matching units before solving more involved journey questions.

Example and related practice

A journey of 120 km in 2 hours has average speed 120 ÷ 2 = 60 km/h.
