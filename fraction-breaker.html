How to Play

Count the colored blocks and the spaces in the grid. In Challenge, count only the color named on screen.

Enter an equivalent fraction and press BREAK!. The original fraction breaks immediately for normal points.

If you make the fraction smaller but do not reach simplest form, you earn a +75 reduction bonus and the grid breaks normally.

Reach simplest form to earn the +150 simplify bonus and the special simplify animation.

Scoring

Each correct grid starts with 100 points, plus 10 points per second remaining and +50 for a first-try solve. Reduction and simplest-form bonuses are added before level and streak multipliers.

Wrong answers reset your streak but do not cost a life. A life is lost only when the timer runs out.
