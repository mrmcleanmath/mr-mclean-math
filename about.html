About Mr. McLean Math

Hi, I’m Mr. McLean, a mathematics teacher focused on making math clear, practical and easier to understand.

I created Mr. McLean Math to give learners a simple place where they can both learn a concept and practice it immediately. The site brings together tutorial videos, quizzes and interactive math apps I have created for use in real classrooms.

My teaching approach focuses on clear explanations, manageable steps and helping students understand why the mathematics works.

For learners, the goal is to make math feel less intimidating. For teachers, the goal is to provide useful resources that students can work with independently.

Mr. McLean Math will continue to grow as new tutorials and practice activities are added.
