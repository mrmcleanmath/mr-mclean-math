Mr. McLean Math — Showcase Mode (v286)

Showcase Mode only activates if ?showcase=1 is in the page URL.
The ordinary Practice and Games pages are unchanged.

After publishing this ZIP to your website, open these links on your phone:

PRACTICE (automatically displays all apps, grouped by subject):
https://www.mrmcleanmath.com/practice.html?showcase=1

GAMES (scrolls through the current Games page):
https://www.mrmcleanmath.com/games.html?showcase=1

To make a recording:
1. Open one of the Showcase links on your phone.
2. Select Slow, Medium, or Fast scrolling speed (Medium is the default).
3. Begin your phone's screen recording.
4. Tap Start scrolling. After a brief opening moment, the site scrolls at a constant speed and the controls disappear.
5. Tap anywhere on the screen to pause and show the controls; tap Resume to continue.
6. Use Restart to begin again, or tap View games/practice library to switch pages.
7. Scrolling stops automatically at the bottom; tap to bring back the controls.
8. Use Exit to return to the ordinary public page.

Only games.html, practice.html, and VERSION.txt were modified from the uploaded ZIP.
Two new files, showcase-mode.js and showcase-mode.css, contain the feature.
All other website files were preserved.
