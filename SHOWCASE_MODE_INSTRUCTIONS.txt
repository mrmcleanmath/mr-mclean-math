Mr. McLean Math — Showcase Mode (v287)

Showcase Mode only activates if ?showcase=1 is in the page URL.
The ordinary Practice and Games pages are unchanged.

After publishing this ZIP to your website, open these links on your phone:

PRACTICE (automatically displays all apps, grouped by subject):
https://www.mrmcleanmath.com/practice.html?showcase=1

GAMES (scrolls through the current Games page):
https://www.mrmcleanmath.com/games.html?showcase=1

To make a recording:
1. Open either Showcase link on your phone.
2. Choose Scroll Direction: Top to bottom (default), or Bottom to top.
   - Bottom to top moves the page to the very bottom before your recording starts.
3. Choose Slow, Medium, or Fast scrolling speed (Medium is default).
4. Begin your phone's screen recording.
5. Tap Start scrolling. After a brief opening moment, the site scrolls smoothly
   in your chosen direction, and the controls disappear.
6. Tap anywhere on the screen to pause and show the controls; tap Resume to continue.
7. Tap Restart to start from the selected direction's starting edge.
   You may also change direction while paused, which resets to the appropriate edge.
8. Scrolling stops automatically when it reaches the other edge.
   Tap once to bring back the controls, then replay if desired.
9. Use View games/practice library to switch pages, or Exit to browse normally.

This feature is active only with ?showcase=1. All other website functionality is preserved.
