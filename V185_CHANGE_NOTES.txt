Mr. McLean Math v185

Solving Equations with Brackets
- Foundation Mode now keeps the variable on one side of the equation only, so students can focus on expanding brackets, simplifying, and isolating the variable.
- Core Mode now introduces variables on both sides while keeping the bracket structure more straightforward.
- Challenge Mode remains the place for the more complex two-sided / multiple-bracket equations.
- The v184 rule preventing negative division in Foundation and Core remains unchanged.
